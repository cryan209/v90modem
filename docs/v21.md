# V.21 and Bell 103 300-bit/s modes

`AT+MS=V21,0,300,300` selects fixed V.21, and `AT+MS=B103,0,300,300` selects
Bell 103. `--mode v21` / `--mode b103` set the power-on offer.

The vendored SpanDSP FSK presets supply both modes. They are one engine path
(`ME_MOD_V21` / `ME_MOD_BELL103`) that differs only in its tones:

| | Caller (originate) | Answerer |
|---|---|---|
| V.21 §3, §7(a) | channel 1: 980 Hz mark, 1180 Hz space | channel 2: 1650 Hz mark, 1850 Hz space |
| Bell 103 | 1270 Hz mark, 1070 Hz space | 2225 Hz mark, 2025 Hz space |

The bit rate is 300 bit/s, and asynchronous bits follow V.21 §4. A Bell 103
answerer's answer tone is its own 2225 Hz mark, so starting both channels
when media connects is what a Bell 103 answerer does anyway.

Neither mode has V.8 or V.25 answer-tone negotiation. Both ends must select
the same family out of band.

## Carrier qualification (circuit 109)

SpanDSP's FSK carrier detect measures broadband power. On its own it reported
carrier about 20 ms after *any* signal, so the two families connected to each
other and printed garbage. Ringback or speech would do the same.

V.21 §8.2.2 Table 2 gives circuit 109 OFF-to-ON as 300-700 ms on the switched
network. The engine therefore reports CONNECT only after the receive channel's
two characteristic frequencies have carried at least half of the received
power, in every 20 ms block, for 400 ms without a break.

- Each block is two Goertzel bins against the block energy.
- The bins are 50 Hz wide, so Bell 103 and V.21 channel 1, whose tones are
  90 Hz apart, stay distinct.

Measured in `engine_pair_test`:

- A matched pair connects at 0.40 s in both modes and both laws.
- Bell 103 against V.21 never connects (`--expect-no-connect`).
- Such a call is ended by S7 on a live line. The pair harness runs on
  simulated time, so it cannot exercise S7.

## Data path

The GUI local echo profiles (`v21`, `b103`) use buffered V.14 framing. The
ordinary engine keeps the configured link policy; use `AT+ES=0,0,0` for
asynchronous data. FSK transmit and receive state is released on disconnect
and recreated per call. Idle transmission is mark, and losing carrier after
CONNECT hangs up. No sample buffering or conversion is added.

The engine-pair regressions and the local SIP GUI echo
(`tools/gui_loopback_test.py b103 v21`) return binary payloads that include
every byte value. The GUI test collects output as it arrives; its bounded
256-chunk display could otherwise discard the start of a long, slow stream
before the whole pattern finishes.

Still open: V.23 (1200/75; the FSK presets exist), and V.25 automode detection of a
V.21 or Bell 103 caller. Bell 212A (`docs/bell212a.md`) answers a Bell 103
caller by falling back to this path.
