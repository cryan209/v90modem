# Eicon V.34 fax test, 8 October 2026

Live PCMU calls from `sip_v90_modem` in Tower's existing
`v90modem-sip` Docker container to Eicon number 7910 now reach V.34 fax
startup and page transmission after the training-power fix below.
**No page has transferred successfully.** The Eicon reports primary-channel
HDLC FCS errors even when its input waveform matches our transmit tap.
The first call and subsequent diagnostic trials are recorded separately below.

## Numbers and channels

The live `/etc/bri-test/config.json` maps:

| Number | Law | Diva controller / BRI port | Modem devices |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7900 | PCMA | 2 | ttyds1, ttyds2 |
| 7910 | PCMU | 1 | ttyds3, ttyds4 |

Each number serves **both channels on its port**, rather than one modem.
Either device can receive a call to that number. For this test both 7910
devices were temporarily handed to HylaFAX; the four-channel data menu
configuration was restored afterward and all four devices reported ready.

## Fax handler and test

Eicon's `AT+FCLASS=?` returns `0,1,1.0,2`. Debian HylaFAX 6.0.7 was installed
on Eicon and two temporary `faxgetty` services used Class 1.0 with ECM and
`AT+F34=14,1,2`. HylaFAX owns T.30 and image reception. It must use
`ModemRaiseATCommands: no`: Diva's `AT+iQ=a1` controller binding is case
sensitive. Controller binding, accepted number and caller ID commands must
be separate commands, using a quoted `ModemResetCmds` string with `\n`.

Tower used the existing isolated build `/tmp/eicon-current-20261008`,
SIP account 2900, SIP/RTP ports 5078/14600, PCMU, and Class 2.1 with
`AT+FIS=1,3,0,2,0,1,0,0,0` (9600-bit/s ceiling, ECM).
The DTE waits for `+FCO`, then `+FDT`/`CONNECT` before sending its A4 page.
No page bytes were supplied because the connection gate never opened.
The fixed jitter buffer remained at its normal default.

Binary SHA-256:
`53c28c3f44a51d3a46a38c6a63bdf5072ad0228c5415473047110e0b28b925e2`.

## Observed boundary

Our trace reports successful V.8 at +4.109 s and source-role V.34 HDX.
It progresses through INFOH, S/S-bar and TRN, then the control-channel stages,
and enters stage 73 (HDX_AC recovery) at +13.468 s. No `+FCO` is emitted.
The Eicon returns plain `CONNECT` about 26 seconds after answering, without
a `+F34` rate indication, and HylaFAX begins legacy V.21 NSF/CSI/DIS and
`AT+FRH=3`. Thus the two ends have not established the same V.34 session.
At the time of the first call the exact cause was unresolved; stage transitions alone do not prove the
peer decoded our training or that our receiver decoded the peer correctly.

The Tower production-engine regression
`./fax_class2_test --engine-class21 ulaw 160` completed with
`Annex F: 0 failures`, including A4 transmit/receive, multipage calls and
raster comparisons. That loopback result does not establish Eicon interop.

Local evidence is under `artifacts/eicon-fax-20261008/`: sender/encoder
scripts, Tower regression log, Eicon session/journal logs, and the live
modem log, serial transcript, DS0 taps and I/O schedule. These artifacts
are gitignored. The temporary Eicon fax services were stopped; HylaFAX
remains installed with its test configurations for a future retry. Unused
HylaFAX packages on Tower were removed after the custom DTE was used.

## Diagnosed training-power defect and hardware retests

Independent binary-DPSK decoding of the Eicon RX tap finds a CRC-valid
Table 22 INFOh at approximately 5.598 seconds: power reduction 0 dB,
TRN duration 4445 ms, high carrier, pre-emphasis 0, baud index 5 (3429),
and **16-point TRN**. PP correlates at 0.913 with the independent
10.1.3.6 sequence. The subsequent four-second TRN interval has RMS
10080.97 PCM units, while the control-channel transmission has RMS 3224.51.

The Phase 3 TRN generator returned the raw 16-point coordinate table,
whose mean symbol energy is ten times the normalized four-point table's.
It did not compensate for the constellation change. V.34 10.2.3 requires
the selected transmit power for all Phase 3 signals. Applying 1/sqrt(10)
to the generator output, without changing the coordinate table, makes the
same live interval RMS **3187.86**: a 10.00 dB reduction.

On the original tap, the Eicon sends sustained 2400 Hz Tone A after our
TRN. Under 12.3.3 this requests primary-channel training recovery rather
than accepting the transition to the control channel. On corrected call
`eicon-fax-live-20261008-r2`, it instead sends PPh and CRC-valid Type-1 MPh
frames. This live behavioral change confirms that the power error was the
first blocker. The new generator regression measures long scrambled TRN
sequences in both constellation sizes and checks their mean energy against
the selected symbol RMS. It passes on Tower and macOS. The 3429/9600 PCMU
HDX control-channel loopback also has zero payload bit errors both ways.

Call r2 still has repeated control-channel recovery with the Eicon configured
for preferred 2400-bit/s control (`AT+F34=14,1,2`). Independent decoding
finds CRC-valid source Type-0 and recipient Type-1 MPh frames on the two DS0
taps; this is not evidence that the Eicon accepted the source's offer.
Changing only the Eicon preference to `AT+F34=14,1,1` on call r3 produces
an actual **`+F34:4,1` / V.34 9600 primary, 1200 control** indication.
HylaFAX receives the correct `V90MODEM LAB` TSI and the DCS, then sends CFR.
The source DTE receives `+FCO`, `+FCS` and `CONNECT` and supplies its page.
Thus V.34 startup and T.30 phase B now work against this foreign modem.
The exact cause of the preferred-2400 control mismatch remains unresolved;
1200 is a tested configuration, not a protocol fix for it.

**The page still fails.** After switching to the primary channel the Eicon
reports repeated HDLC FCS errors. Our Class 2.1 response ultimately contains
`+FHS:50`; a following `OK` does not make this a successful fax. The r3
custom sender's `page_sent: true` summary only records handing the bytes to
the modem and observing `OK`; use the serial hangup status and peer log as
the authoritative failure evidence. The primary-channel corruption is a
separate remaining interop problem, above the now-confirmed training-power
defect. No successfully received page or image comparison is claimed.

The r2/r3 captures, independent MPh decoder, replay logs, and peer session
logs are preserved beside the first-call evidence. All four Eicon data-menu
channels were restored after these retests. No Type-1 source MPh experiment
was applied: the tested source retains its original Type-0 offer.
The corrected live binary SHA-256 is
`1c56504a3bd260db2a5a44df8951e38ab110e485d308e7942a6e725e2b206e1f`.
The corrected Tower production Class 2.1 engine page regression also finishes
with `Annex F: 0 failures`, including both page directions, multipage calls,
and post-CONNECT page submission; its log is `eicon-fax-power-engine.log`.

## Follow-up: intact primary delivery, 8 October evening

The 3429/9600 PCMU primary loopback passes with zero errors. Its normal
configuration does not request nonlinear encoding, whereas the Eicon does.
`V34_HDX_NON_LINEAR=1` now lets the recipient request Table 23 bit 31's
9.7 nonlinear encoder in the production HDX harness. Four rows (both laws,
source as calling and answering modem) each grade 65200 primary bits with
zero errors and require that the negotiated transmitter actually enables
the requested encoder. These rows are included in `v34-hdx-primary-test`.
This closes a coverage gap; it does not establish hardware conformance.

Independent correlation of r3's primary PP against 10.1.3.6 is 0.9149,
starting at TX-tap sample 213788 (26.7235 s). The engine's post-V.8 sample
marks have a different origin from the complete tap; using those marks as
absolute file offsets would give a false low correlation.

A repeat with original Type-0 MPh, r4, stalls before page CONNECT despite
both devices using `AT+F34=14,1,1`. Card Audio1 samples match local TX from
6.000 through 83.6505 seconds at card-minus-TX offset 556 samples: 621204
identical decoded waveform samples, with only 194 zero-polarity codeword
changes. Independent decoding finds valid MPh in both directions, with
both ends selecting 1200-bit/s control. Thus this startup stall is present
with intact delivery, not explained by the previously seen tunnel loss.

A diagnostic Type-1 source MPh with zero coefficients was then tested in
the isolated Tower build. Clause 10.2.4.4 allows either type. The first
attempt, r5, dialed before faxgetty initialization finished and its redial
ran into the DTE timeout; it provides no usable MPh interop result.
The clean r6 retry waits for both fax handlers to report RUNNING. Its
actual TX tap contains CRC-valid Type-1 frames. Eicon reports `+F34:4,1`,
accepts TSI/DCS, sends CFR, selects primary, and again reports repeated
FCS errors. The sender records `+FHS:50` and `page_sent: false`. Type-1
therefore does not cure primary corruption; its longer startup is not
evidence of an improvement over Type-0.

The r6 Audio1 trace matches every decoded TX waveform sample from 25.000
through 97.792 seconds at constant offset 527: **582336 samples with zero
waveform errors or sample shifts**, including the primary transmission
and the Eicon's CRC-error interval. There are 177 alternate zero codewords.
This directly excludes waveform corruption between the local tap and card
input over the measured interval. The remaining primary encoder/acquisition
interop fault is unresolved; matching our own receiver is insufficient to
locate it. Independent mapping and B1 verification at the fractional
3429-baud profile is the next diagnostic boundary.

All traces and comparison JSON are preserved under the same artifact root.
The Tower Type-1 source edit was reverted and the original build relinked.
All four Eicon data handlers were restored and the service verified active.

## Independent mapper and burst boundaries, 8 October night

The independent encoder oracle now supports the 3429/9600 fractional profile,
the GPC scrambler, and captures which include B1. It matches 28304 raw symbols
from the production HDX path, including B1, with nonlinear encoding enabled;
standalone 3200/9600 and 3429/9600 fixtures also match. Flipping an input bit
makes the comparison fail. This rules out a broad local mapping disagreement
with the independently implemented clauses 7, 8.2 and 9.3--9.6, but does not
prove the Eicon's receiver acquires the transmitted primary signal.

Two burst defects were corrected. The external V.34 fax path queued only two
HDLC flags before ECM data (1.67 ms at 9600 bit/s). T.30 F.3.2.3/F.3.4 refer
to T.4 A.3.1's nominal 200 ms preamble; it now queues a rate-dependent 200 ms
of flags. A regression checks the actual fax transmitter at four primary
rates and verifies that the first frame follows those flags. Also, the
12.4.1.1 70 ms silence bypassed the pulse shaper, leaving primary TRN history
inside the subsequent 600-baud PPh burst. The transition now clears that
history; a regression verifies identical PPh waveforms after clean and dirty
primary histories. Continuous control-channel transitions are unchanged.

The complete production Class 2.1 page regression passes after the preamble
change, with exact received rasters and `Annex F: 0 failures`. After the PPh
change, production negotiation passes in both directions, and all four
3429/9600 nonlinear primary loopback rows still carry 65200 error-free bits.
Opt-in `V34_PRIMARY_TX_BIT_DUMP` records the actual scrambler input, including
B1, for a future live primary comparison.

Hardware attempts r7--r11 did not reach page CONNECT. The r8 diagnostic
withdrawal of 3429 support elicited CRC-valid INFOh selecting 3200 low carrier,
but did not reach primary data. Removing the existing E-wait delay in r10
also did not help. The r11 PPh-history fix still leaves repeated control
startup attempts; HylaFAX sees only a legacy CONNECT at 82 seconds, without
`+F34`, then NO CARRIER. These calls provide no hardware validation of the
new ECM preamble, and no successfully received page is claimed.

The diagnostic Type-1 MPh edits remain outside the committed source. The
isolated Tower build was restored to its normal Type-0 offer and rebuilt.
The Eicon configuration matches its pre-fax backup, all four data handlers
are restored, and `bri-test-answerer` is active. Latest traces and logs are
preserved under `artifacts/eicon-fax-20261008/`. The unresolved boundaries
are intermittent control-channel startup and primary acquisition/FCS errors
on calls which do complete startup.
